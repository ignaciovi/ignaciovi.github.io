Hey there, I'm Nacho. Welcome to my wall!

I started this website as an exercise to make public the multiple thoughts and learnings I've been writing down in the last few years and gathering in a private space. This will also include new stuff that I'm learning. The objective of this exercise is to get used to writing to an audience that is not just me, since I believe this will help me improve my writing and communication skills.

I've decided to write my notes here and not in a publishing website like Medium or Substack for two reasons:
1. I like the idea of having my own website. Having a personal space is a concept that was popular not so long ago, when Myspace was still a thing. Everyone had their own little personalised website they customised without any preconception of how a website should look like. I want to write about anything I want without being constrained by a category.
2. I want to be able to modify previous notes, and that's something that I can't do in the publishing websites I mentioned earlier. In some occassions, I like reading my past posts and being embarrased about what I wrote. That useful for me to see how much I've changed. Other times, I like to review those posts and modify them if I have new information to add or improvements to make.

That said, in this "wall" you will see a mix of notes/articles that I'll do my best to organise using tags. As said before, this project will be a mix bag of concepts, learnings and other stuff I've been collecting and still collect. As I write, I'll hopefully improve my communication skills by pure practice, and have a place that I can come back when I want to relearn something or just wander around.

I don't want to finish without giving credit to [jackyzha0](https://github.com/jackyzha0). Not sure how, I came across his Github page, and his own personal website and the Quartz repo have greatly inspired me to start this project. Enjoy!
